Two ways to clear it out

You load

Dump trailer drop-off

Best for remodels, yard projects and move-outs where you want to work at your own pace.

  • We deliver the trailer to your driveway or job site
  • Load it over a few days
  • We haul it to the dump or transfer station and recycle what we can
  • Weight limits apply to heavy material like dirt, concrete and roofing
We load

Full-service junk removal

Best for garages, furniture, appliances and cleanouts when you don't want to lift a thing.

  • We arrive, load and sweep up
  • Priced by how much of the trailer you fill
  • Usable furniture goes to donation where possible
  • Single items to whole-house cleanouts

What we take

Yes

  • Furniture, mattresses and household junk
  • Remodel debris: drywall, wood, cabinets, flooring
  • Yard waste, branches and old fencing
  • Garage and storage-unit cleanouts
  • Appliances (ask us about fridges and AC units)

No

  • Paint, solvents, oil and chemicals
  • Batteries and propane tanks
  • Tires
  • Asbestos or anything that may contain it
  • Medical waste

Questions

How long can I keep the trailer?

Rentals are quoted by the day or the week. Tell us your project and we'll suggest the shortest rental that fits.

Will it damage my driveway?

We set boards under the jack stand and park on a level spot you choose. Let us know about soft ground, slopes or tight gates when you book.

Can I fill it with dirt or concrete?

Heavy material fills the trailer's weight limit long before it fills the space. Tell us what you're loading and we'll tell you how high to fill it.
